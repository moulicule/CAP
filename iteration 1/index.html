The Surveillance Dilemma

Smart glasses look virtually indistinguishable from regular designer frames, yet pack cameras and microphones right at eye level. They record people in public and private spaces without consent, while indicator LEDs are easily missed or covered up.

Tactical Defense (CaP)

Smart glasses can’t hide their radio transmissions. To sync with phones, they constantly broadcast Bluetooth LE advertisement packets with vendor IDs. CaP intercepts these airwaves to warn you immediately through an expressive companion.

How It’s Built

Hardware, firmware, and industrial design

Hardware Stack

ESP32-S3 + Round LCD

Powered by a Waveshare ESP32-S3-Zero and a 1.28″ round 240×240 GC9A01 LCD running at 30 FPS over 40MHz SPI via DMA. Includes an ADXL345 accelerometer for knock detection and an ERM haptic motor.

Firmware Engine

Dual-Core Concurrency

Core 0 runs a continuous 100% duty-cycle NimBLE scanner tracking Bluetooth SIG vendor IDs (Meta 0x01AB/0x058E, Snap 0x03C2, Vuzix). Core 1 runs the spring physics render pipeline.

Physical Enclosure

3D-Printed Squircle

Designed in Fusion 360 and 3D printed in safety orange. Built to snap securely shut with support for the round display, an integrated battery, USB-C recharge port, and power switch.
